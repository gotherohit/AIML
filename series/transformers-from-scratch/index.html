Transformers from scratch

We open up one real model, GPT-2 small, one box at a time, and follow one sentence, "The cat sat on", all the way to the next word. Then how it learned, how it writes, and what newer models changed. Every number is computed on the real model, and in the notebook you write each step yourself.

Start with Episode 1 Code on GitHub Subscribe on YouTube

How the series works

Episodes

Each episode takes one idea all the way down to the matrix shapes, on the real GPT-2. A video walks through it with the notebook; the article has every number, the 3-D views and the parts the video skips.

Notebooks

One Colab notebook per chapter, each with every earlier chapter, so the newest has everything. You fill in the blanks and a check cell tells you when your numbers match GPT-2. gotherohit/llm-scratch

TinyGPT, our own model

The last section of every chapter adds that chapter's piece to our own PyTorch model. It starts from random weights and, in the training episodes, is trained from scratch on the TinyStories dataset.

Deep dives

Short side videos (D1.1, D1.2 …) for background an episode only touches on, like 3-D matrix multiplication. You don't need them to follow along.
